Live Subtitles

Pick the language being spoken and the language you want, then tap Start listening.

Settings

Play the radio or TV and watch the bar. Move the phone until it stays green while people speak.
  • Put the phone close to the speaker, about an arm's length away. The microphone is at the bottom of the phone, so point the bottom at the sound.
  • Turn the volume up a little. Clear voices work much better than voices over loud music.
  • If your TV or soundbar has a "clear voice" or "dialogue" setting, turn it on.
  • For the best results, plug a small clip-on microphone (a wired "lavalier" mic) into the phone and put it right next to the TV speaker. The phone then hears the TV as if someone were talking close to it. iPhones need a headphone adapter for this.
  • Keep people near the phone quiet. The phone listens to the loudest voice in the room.
  • On iPhone, if a "Mic Mode" button shows in Control Center while the app listens, pick "Wide Spectrum".
MyMemory allows about 5,000 characters a day without an email, and about 50,000 with one. The email is only sent to MyMemory and stays on this phone.
